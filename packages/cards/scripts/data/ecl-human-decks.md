# Lorwyn Eclipsed (ECL) human-made decklists, by colour pair

Research notes for later deck building. Every list below was read from a page (MTG Arena Zone, CoolStuffInc). Nothing is reconstructed from memory. Counts are as printed on the source page.

**"Not from ECL"** means the card's earliest Scryfall printing is not in set ECL (checked via `api.scryfall.com` `unique=prints`, order=released asc). Reprints that first appeared in ECL count as ECL. Basic lands are shown separately and not counted.

Format note: the five Arena Zone lists are limited archetype skeletons (restricted, not tuned trophy decks). The CoolStuffInc lists are 60-card Standard constructed brews by one columnist; they are the only Standard decks found with full lists. No 17Lands, MTGGoldfish, Untapped or Wizards decklists were accessible.
