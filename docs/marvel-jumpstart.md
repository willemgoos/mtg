# Marvel Super Heroes Jumpstart packets

To resume this work, start with `docs/marvel-jumpstart-handoff.md`.

Tracking the 51 official Marvel Super Heroes Jumpstart half-decks (paper Jumpstart Boosters, also on Arena as Jumpstart events), from
[Wizards' theme list](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/marvel-super-heroes-jumpstart-booster-themes).
Each packet is 13 cards plus 7 basics (Fantastic has its own lands). A packet goes in once every card is implemented.

Totals (October 2026): 291 cards missing across all packets; 227 are new to Magic (most made for Jumpstart), 64 are reprints
(Lightning Bolt, Dark Ritual, Doom Blade, Time Warp, the Thriving lands, cards from Marvel's Spider-Man and others).

## Shared cards first

- The five **Thriving lands** (Heath, Isle, Moor, Bluff, Grove) are in almost every packet: done first, in `packages/cards/src/msh/jumpstart.ts`.
- Then, by how many packets use them: Marvelous Melee and Colossal Collision (5); Heroic Teamwork, Fall to Earth, Quantum
  Reduction, Project Deathlok Soldier, Gert and Old Lace, Accelerated Evolution (4).

## Done so far

- **HYDRA, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Trained, Pym Particles, Animal, Heroes for Hire, Great Lakes Avengers,
  Tricksters, Masters of Evil, Thor, Lethal, Soaring, Incredible, Scarlet, Wild, Tenacious, Rampaging, Wakanda, Atlantis, Geniuses, Marvelous,
  Young Avengers, Towering, Precise, Kang Dynasty, Equipped,
  Squadron, Conniving, Battalion, Analyzed and Blink** are in `MARVEL_JUMPSTART_PACKETS` (`packages/cards/src/jumpin.ts`), shown in the
  Jump In picker as "Marvel · Jumpstart". Cards: `packages/cards/src/msh/jumpstart*.ts`, tests in
  `packages/cards/test/msh-jumpstart*.test.ts`.
- Engine pieces they added: a filter for "attacking alone" (Viper), "it" after a card is put onto the battlefield from hand
  (Nick Fury), "your first power-up each turn costs {0}" (Advancing the Spirit), and doubling +1/+1 counters on your
  creatures (She-Hulk); teamwork that grants flash (Quantum Reduction), "whenever this becomes tapped" (Wakandan Tusker), an extra
  Food whenever you make tokens (Tippy-Toe), and a condition for "if an up-to target was chosen" (Iron Fist, Hero for Hire); abilities from exile (Mister Immortal), "can't be blocked by" and switching power and toughness until end of
  turn (Doorman, Flatman), copies that aren't legendary or keep their name (Loki, Impossible Man), emblems that watch a named
  creature (The Clone Saga), "tap an untapped Villain" as a cost (Villainous Syndication) and half the opponent's life
  (Radioactive Man), noncombat-damage triggers from the graveyard (Asgardian Inspiration) and creature cards in
  the opponent's graveyard (Origin of Black Widow), storm through "when you cast this spell" (Grapeshot), "your second spell each turn",
  playing exiled cards until your next end step (Wiccan, Wanda's Vision), exiling a hand to draw that many (Hex Magic),
  behold as a free kicker and counter doubling for one creature type (Hulk's Thunderclap, Hulk), "its activated abilities can't be activated" (Secure Detention), entering with a counted
  number of counters and creature cards put into your graveyard (Voracious Brood), "whenever this blocks" (Atlas), fights that
  check excess damage (Rhino's Rampage) and a counter of each kind (Powerful Broker), a first-extra-draw replacement and upkeep triggers for the enchanted creature's controller (Reed
  Richards, Super Intelligence), "whenever you activate a power-up" and "another creature is exiled" (Marvel Boy, Captain
  Marvel), power above base power (Ms. Marvel), copies that keep some of their own abilities (Hulkling), "a spell that targets any creature" (Hawkeye,
  Bowslinger) and shuffling hand and graveyard into the library (Immortus), "prevent all but 1 damage" (Hyperion), battalion (three or more attackers), "any number of targets" picked
  one at a time (Silver Surfer), cast triggers for teamwork spells, copying an ability from a creature source (Echo),
  cards playable while you control a permanent (Victor Mancha) and a free cast once each turn (Vision).
- Simplifications (also in `docs/shortcuts.md`):
  - Advancing the Spirit's free power-up isn't optional.
